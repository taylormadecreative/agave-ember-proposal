What comes next

These are in the order I’d do them, and you don’t need everything at once. Tap an add button on anything that feels right, and you can send me your list at the end.

First

Elevate the food

You want the food looking its best before a photo shoot happens. So I’d start with AI images now, then replace them with real photos when you’re ready.

  • AI images now: I start from your own dish photos and improve the lighting, plating and setting, so each dish still looks like what guests get.
  • New dishes: the new steaks and the Rubi Crown Salmon don’t have photos on the site yet, so I’d need a quick phone photo of each from your kitchen.
  • AI food video: short moving shots of your dishes for the website and social media.
  • Real photos and video later: I shoot your dishes, drinks and dining room at the restaurant, and they replace the AI images.

We choose the dishes together, and you approve every image before it goes up.

Second

Go live on agave-ember.com

Right now your domain says Coming Soon. This step puts the new site there and opens it to Google. The search and tracking steps depend on it.

  • Your domain points to the new site and stays where it is.
  • Reservation requests go where you choose.
  • Your phone number, hours and address are set across the site.
Third

Get found on Google and Maps

This is local SEO, which means showing up when someone nearby searches for what you serve, like “Mexican restaurant in Allen” or “brunch in Allen.”

  • Google Business Profile: the listing with your hours, photos and directions that appears in Google Search and Maps.
  • Apple Maps and Yelp: the same name, address, hours and phone number everywhere.
  • Your website’s wording: page titles and text written for the searches guests make.

Nobody can promise the top spot on Google. This is the work that gives you a real shot at it.

Fourth

See what’s working

Tracking shows where your guests come from, so you know what’s worth doing again. It goes in the day the site goes live, so your first visitors count.

  • Google Analytics and Search Console: Google tools that show how many people visit and what they searched to find you.
  • Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel: small pieces of code that show who came from Facebook, Instagram or TikTok, and let you reach them with ads later if you choose.
  • Tap counts: how often people tap Reserve, Directions, Call and Menu.
  • Labeled links and QR codes: one for your social bios and one for each printed piece, so you can tell which brought people in.
  • A one-page report every month, plus the privacy page these tools expect.

Everything is set up in your own accounts, with me added as an admin. It stays yours.

Fifth

Run ads

Ads put your food in front of people nearby who haven’t found you yet. With tracking in place, you can see which ads lead to taps on Reserve, Directions and Call.

  • Facebook and Instagram: ads that show your food to people near the restaurant.
  • Google: ads that appear when someone nearby searches for Mexican food or brunch.
  • Built around what you want to push: brunch, a new dish or a private event.
  • A plain report on what each ad brought in.

We set the budget together, and you approve every ad before it runs.

Every month

Keep it current

Menus change, and yours already has since the site was built. Send me the change and I update it, so guests see the right dish at the right price.

  • Menu, price and hours changes.
  • Specials, events and holiday hours.
  • New photos swapped in after a shoot.

What I’d need from you

  • The login for agave-ember.com, or whoever can make the change with me.
  • The address, phone number and hours you want shown everywhere.
  • Where reservation requests should go.
  • If you run ads, a monthly budget you’re comfortable with.
  • Access to your Google, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok accounts. If one doesn’t exist yet, we’ll set it up in your name.
